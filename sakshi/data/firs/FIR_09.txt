On 29/09/2026 at 3:00 PM, Shri Lakhan Sinha, an autorickshaw driver at Durg railway station, gave a statement to the police. He stated that a man known locally as Bhura or Bhura bhaiya, whose real name he believes is Suresh, arranges rides and rooms for young girls who come from villages looking for work. He often sees Bhura bhaiya moving in a black Scorpio bearing number cg 04 yh 7615 near the railway station and Hotel Raj Palace. On 27/09/2026 at night he saw Bhura bhaiya talking on phone +91 87710-93248 near the station and then driving away towards the highway. Usne bataya ki Bhura bhaiya ke saath kabhi kabhi do-teen log aur hote hain jo alag alag gaadiyon se aate hain. The witness is willing to identify Bhura bhaiya if shown. The statement was recorded and the witness was asked to inform the police if he sees the person again. The area beat staff have been asked to check the mentioned vehicle.
