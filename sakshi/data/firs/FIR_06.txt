On 28/09/2026 at about 10:20 AM, Shri Mohan Lal Tandon, security guard of a commercial complex near Bhilai Civic Center, informed the police about a suspicious vehicle. He stated that on 27/09/2026 around 5:05 PM a white Bolero bearing registration CG04MG5088 remained parked near the Civic Center for almost an hour with its engine running. Two men were sitting in the front seats and kept looking at the girls passing through the market. When he asked them to move, the driver said he was waiting for a passenger and drove towards Supela. The guard noted the number as CG04MG5088 in his duty diary. He could not identify the men but said the driver was heavily built and wore a grey cap. The complainant offered to show the diary entry and the complex CCTV footage to the police. The vehicle number has been sent to the toll plaza and traffic police for checking.
