Neha was wearing a yellow kurti with blue jeans and carrying a black sling bag. Ghar waalon ne uske mobile +91 73662-99468 par baar baar call kiya, lekin phone switch off aa raha hai. Neha ki saheli ne bataya ki woh is number par do-teen baar baat kar chuki thi aur ek baar interview ke liye bulaya gaya tha. No ransom or threatening call has been received by the family so far.
